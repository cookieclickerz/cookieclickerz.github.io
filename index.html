📖 About

What is Cookie Clicker?

Cookie Clicker is the original idle clicker game that started a genre. First released in 2013, it pioneered the concept of incremental games where players click a cookie to earn more cookies, then use those cookies to buy buildings and upgrades that generate cookies automatically — even when you're not actively playing.

The genius of Cookie Clicker lies in its infinite scalability. What starts as clicking a simple cookie grows into managing a vast empire of grandmas, factories, time machines, and interdimensional portals — all dedicated to the singular goal of baking as many cookies as possible.

Today, Cookie Clicker remains one of the most beloved free online games with millions of players worldwide.

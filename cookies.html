Cookies Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

While our entire website is dedicated to the virtual art of baking trillions of delicious cookies, we take a highly structured, minimalist approach to actual web technical cookies. This Cookies Policy explains how we utilize local technologies to power your gameplay experience.

1. The Irony: We Don't Use Traditional Tracking Cookies

Cookie Clicker (cookieclickerz.github.io) does not deploy traditional tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or cross-site pixels to monitor your web browsing activity across the internet. We do not build promotional profiles or monitor who you are.

2. Browser Local Storage (Essential Tech)

Instead of server-side tracking cookies, this website relies extensively on browser-side Local Storage. This serves as a local client-side memory vault built directly inside your own web browser.

  • Why it is mandatory: Without Local Storage, the game cannot remember your progress. Every time you refreshed the browser page or returned tomorrow, your entire cookie factory, grandmas, and building points would be completely lost.
  • What it stores: It tracks numerical balances, click metrics, automated production speed settings, unlocked achievement statuses, and active system milestones.
  • Security profile: This architecture stays locked locally inside your personal web browser sandbox and is never transmitted out onto external operational databases or cloud storage infrastructure.

3. Third-Party CDNs and Web Systems

To ensure quick global load speeds, our delivery systems use popular, public content distribution networks (such as Google Fonts) to fetch scripts and style structures. These third-party networks may process baseline technical metrics automatically to provide diagnostic security tracking or infrastructure load balancing.

4. Managing Your Preferences

If you prefer not to store any persistent game states on your device, you can clear your browser's history and application cache at any time, or click the native 🔄 Reset button inside the main panel interface. You can also block local data persistence by browsing our game through an explicit Incognito or Private Windows session.

5. Updates and Contact

We may modify this policy configuration occasionally to adapt to new development features. For clarity or concerns regarding user privacy tools, contact us at our support mailbox.
